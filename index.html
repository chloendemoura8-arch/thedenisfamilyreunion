Our Story

One family, many branches

The Denis family tree has grown wide and far — across cities, across generations, across every road that carried us forward. Yet the roots are the same. This reunion is our chance to gather them all in one place, to put faces to names, to let the children meet their cousins, and to honor the ones who came before us.

Over three days this summer we'll laugh, dance, eat, remember, and make new memories worth keeping. Whether you're traveling near or far, there is a seat at this table with your name on it.
